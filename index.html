FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries about our virtual internships.

Are DevConnect virtual internships 100% online?
Yes! All DevConnect virtual internship programs are 100% online and self-paced. You can work from anywhere in India or internationally according to your convenient timing.
When do I receive my Offer Letter?
Once your enrollment form is submitted, your ISO & MSME verified Offer Letter is generated instantly and available in your student portal as well as sent to your registered email address.
How are certificates verified by hiring companies?
Every certificate contains a unique Credential ID and QR code. Employers can scan the QR code or enter the ID on our live Verification Portal (devconnect.in/verify) to verify candidate details instantly.
Is there mentor support if I get stuck on a coding task?
Yes! You will get access to our active Developer Community on Discord/WhatsApp where mentors and community leads assist you with debugging, code structure, and git submission workflows.
Who is eligible to apply for DevConnect internships?
Students pursuing B.Tech, BE, BCA, MCA, B.Sc Computer Science, IT, Diploma, or recent graduates passionate about learning software development are eligible to apply.
